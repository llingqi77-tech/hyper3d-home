## Design Context

### Users
The homeowner and visitors explore a faithful desktop browser model of the dining and living rooms, starting at the front door and inspecting the plan and individual objects.

### Brand Personality
Warm, calm, lived-in, and personal. The interior is the focus; controls should be readable without competing with the scene.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the supplied floor plan and photographs as the authority. Preserve the dining GLB assets, terracotta floor, wood furniture, floral sofas, cream walls, green desk, and arched forms. Extend the existing restrained cream-and-olive interface.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the verified dining furniture and entry sequence intact while connecting the living room.
2. Match the provided plan: living room north of the arch, dining room south, door at the southern edge.
3. Support both spatial understanding from above and close inspection from eye level.
4. Keep new rooms modular so the kitchen, bathrooms, and bedrooms can follow later.
